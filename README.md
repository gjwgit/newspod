[NewsPod](https://gjwgit.github.io/newspod/) gathers the news from the
RSS and Atom feeds you choose into one list, newest first, with each
story's source, how long ago it was published, its summary and its
picture. Tap a story to read it in full on its own site, and it moves to
**Viewed**, the latest viewed first, to find again. Pull the list down,
or tap **Refresh**, for the latest. A new install starts with a few well
known feeds, ABC News, BBC World, The Guardian and The Conversation.
Under **Feeds** find more: give a site's address to list the feeds it
offers, or words to search for among the feeds followed on Feedly, and
add those you want. Remove any you do not want. Swipe a story right to
keep it under **Later**, even after its feed drops it, and swipe it
right there once read to move it to **Viewed**. Swipe a story left to
remove it from the news. **Dismissed** lists the stories removed, while
their feeds still have them, and a swipe right brings one back.

There is no account, no tracking and no algorithm deciding what you see:
just the feeds you chose, newest first. Your feeds, and the stories you
keep, view and remove, are kept on your device. Logging in to your own
personal online data store (Pod) is optional. When you do, they are kept
on your Pod too, encrypted, so your phone and your desktop show the same
feeds and the same stories kept for later and viewed, and the removed
stories stay removed on both. Changes made while not logged in reach the
Pod the next time you are. Pods
are an innovation from the
inventor of the World Wide Web, Sir Tim Berners-Lee. For more
information visit the [Solid Project](https://solidproject.org).
**You do not need a Solid Pod to use this app.**

## Installation

See the [Install
Guide](https://gjwgit.github.io/newspod/INSTALL.html) for
installation instructions for any platform.

In a browser most feeds cannot be read: news sites only allow their
own pages to read their feeds. Use NewsPod on a desktop or phone.

## Roadmap

+ Keep which stories have been read on the Pod too, as the feeds and
  the removed stories already are.

+ Mark stories as read, and save stories to read later.

+ Group the news by feed or by topic, and search it.

+ Find a site's feed from the address of the site itself.

+ Import and export the feeds as OPML, to move from another reader.
